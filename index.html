About DTU Health Center

Caring for the DTU Community

The DTU Health Center provides comprehensive primary healthcare, preventive medicine, emergency response, and psychological counseling to over 15,000 students, faculty, and administrative staff at Delhi Technological University.

Equipped with basic diagnostic facilities, minor OT capabilities, an on-campus pharmacy dispensary, and 24/7 dedicated ambulance support, we ensure quality healthcare is always within reach on campus.
