BACKGROUND ANALYSIS

Introduction & Background: The Need for Digital Health

🏥 DTU Healthcare Infrastructure
  • DTU operates a dedicated campus health dispensary staffed by qualified general physicians and specialists.
  • Provides primary checkups, first-aid, routine diagnostics, and essential medications to students free of charge.
  • Departments include General OPD, Dentistry, Orthopedics, Mental Health & Counseling, and Physiotherapy.
  • Historically operated through manual walk-in queues and physical pin-board paper notices.
⚠️ The Campus Communication Gap
  • Information about consulting doctor timings is published on physical notice boards, rarely seen by busy students.
  • Bedridden hostellers suffering from fever, sprains, or viral infections face physical hardship walking across campus.
  • Students have no digital mechanism to check doctor availability or queue status beforehand.
  • A unified, student-friendly digital gateway connects residents to on-campus medical aid effortlessly.
IDENTIFIED INEFFICIENCIES

Problem Statement: Real Campus Healthcare Inefficiencies

1. Low Awareness
  • Students are unaware of available facilities and doctors on campus.
  • Students visit expensive outside private clinics for minor ailments (headache, fever, cold).
  • Notice boards fail to reach digital-first students.
2. Overcrowding
  • Surges during lecture break hours cause unpredictable queues.
  • Doctors face burnout due to un-triaged simultaneous walk-ins.
  • Lack of quotas prevents allocating dedicated time per student.
3. Hostel Gaps
  • Late-night hostel emergencies suffer delays finding ambulance contacts.
  • Quarantined students cannot get clinical guidance without exposing others.
  • Paper prescriptions are easily misplaced or lost.
PROJECT GOALS

Project Objectives & Scope: Solving the Core Challenges

🎯 Objective 1: Unified Transparency
  • Single digital portal showcasing operating hours, doctor bios, cabin locations, and specialized clinical departments.
  • Promote healthy campus living through digital wellness bulletins and preventative care tips.
🗓️ Objective 2: Quota-Controlled Booking
  • Enable campus doctors to publish availability slots with a strict threshold (Max 10 students/session).
  • Eliminate waiting lines by allocating digital tokens and live queue status updates.
📄 Objective 3: Paperless Clinical Suite
  • Allow doctors to draft standardized digital prescriptions with automated dosages and instructions.
  • Facilitate specialist referrals (Dental, Orthopedics, Psychiatry) with clinical findings.
📹 Objective 4: Telehealth & Rapid SOS
  • Equip the portal with an interactive browser-based video consultation room for remote triage.
  • Deploy a one-click hostel ambulance SOS button with simulated sirens and 3-5 minute response tracking.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

System Architecture & Dual-Database Engineering

☁️ Google Cloud Firebase Firestore
  • Real-Time Push: Event listeners stream live updates to student & doctor screens in <10ms.
  • 24×7 Cloud Persistence: Hosted on Google Cloud — data stays permanently live even if the host laptop is shut down.
  • Multi-Device Sync: Instant sync between student smartphones and clinical doctor workstations.
  • Collections: appointments, doctor_slots, prescriptions, campus_notices.
🗄️ Relational Hospital DBMS (SQLite 3)
  • Textbook 3NF Normalization: Clean separation across 11 relational tables (students, doctors, departments, etc.).
  • ACID Guarantees: Atomic transaction control ensuring slot capacities never overflow.
  • Capacity Constraint: CHECK (booked_count <= max_capacity) stops race conditions at the engine level.
  • Server-Sent Events: Local /api/events event stream for real-time notifications.
DEMO & CAPABILITIES

Key Functional Modules & Tested Capabilities

1. Student Portal

Mandatory profile check (Roll No, Branch, Blood Group) and dashboard with upcoming appointments.

2. Quota Booking

Strict 10-student cap per doctor window with symptom questionnaires and automatic lockout when full.

3. Doctor Queue

Live patient queue cards with urgency badges, token counters, and instant Call / Refer / Done actions.

4. Telehealth Room

Browser WebRTC webcam stream with in-call chat, duration timer, and real-time prescription draft pad.

5. Digital Rx & Referrals

Standardized digital prescription writer with printable/PDF export and specialist referral transfer slips.

6. 24×7 Ambulance SOS

One-tap hostel emergency trigger with simulated audio siren, direct driver call, and 3-5 min ETA tracking.
