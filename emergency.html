📋 What to do while waiting for the Ambulance

  1. Stay calm: Panic creates confusion. Take a deep breath and keep someone by the patient's side.
  2. Clear the area: Make sure corridors and building entrances are unobstructed so paramedics can enter with stretchers.
  3. Keep patient comfortable: Loosen tight neckwear or belts. If unconscious but breathing, place them in the side recovery position.
  4. Do NOT give liquids: Never force water, food, or medicines into the mouth of an unconscious or drowsy person.
  5. Keep student ID ready: Keep the student's DTU ID card ready for rapid documentation at the Health Center.

🩹 Quick Campus First Aid Tips

🩸 Bleeding Cut:

Apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth or sterile gauze for 10 minutes without lifting.

🦴 Sprains & Sports Injuries:

Follow R.I.C.E protocol — Rest, Ice (cold pack), Compression, Elevation above heart level.

😵 Fainting / Dizziness:

Lay the person flat on their back and elevate legs 12 inches to restore blood flow to the brain.
